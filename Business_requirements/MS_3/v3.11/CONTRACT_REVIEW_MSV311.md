## 4. Risk notes (non-blocking, backend items reviewed at HEAD `c0a6d61`)

- **RISK (low, UX-only)** – CS-01: a sub-thread's own `status` is never used to filter it out of its main's `sub_threads` list (`app/crud/workspace.py:723-728`, deliberate "nic nie ginie" design). A sub-thread that gets closed while its parent main stays open will keep appearing nested under the open main, and will NOT appear in the "closed" (`view=closed`) top-level listing at all, since only top-level MAIN threads are queried per view. Not a contract mismatch (the schema and the client both handle this shape correctly) – flagging as a product-behavior edge case for `ui-ux-designer`/`backend-api` to confirm is intentional, since no Flutter surface currently exposes closing an individual sub-thread from the nested view (`thread_card.dart`'s `_SubThreadRow` only navigates to `_openSubThread`, it doesn't close it inline).
- **Note, not a risk** – `next_session_timezone` on `GET /users/therapy-schedule` can be `None` (`data.get("time_zone")`, no `"UTC"` fallback) while the `PUT` response always falls back to `"UTC"` (`prefs.time_zone or "UTC"`) at `app/api/v1/endpoints/users.py:1423` vs `:1524`. Both are valid against the `Optional[str]` schema and the nullable Flutter field, so this is not a contract break, just a minor inconsistency `backend-api` may want to align for predictability.
