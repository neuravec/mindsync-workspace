1aAssumptions & reasoning
Design notes
Success only. A check always means "I did it" (good) or "I stayed free of it" (bad). Missed past days stay quietly empty — never red.
Color code. Good = teal fill #339499 · white check. Bad = sunset fill #FFC88A · deep-amber check. Both read as wins; the flame streak treats them identically.
Mock week. Today = Thursday Aug 27. Mon–Wed are history, Fri–Sun dimmed & locked.
Row anatomy. Two lines: name line (type cue · full name · flame streak), then 7×32px day cells (44px hit area). Name never truncates.
Edit mode. Dashed sand frame = the app's "scaffold" language; streak chips hide while editing — no celebrating mid-correction.
