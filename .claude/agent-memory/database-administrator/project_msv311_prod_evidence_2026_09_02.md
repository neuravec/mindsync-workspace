Prod audit 2026-09-02 for MSV3.11 (`Business_requirements/MS_3/v3.11/MSV3.11 - Change Spec (Grace).md`). Full report: `db-reports/2026-09-02-msv311-prod-evidence.md`. Used the PowerShell `--no-tty` access variant confirmed 2026-08-16 - this run additionally confirmed **the dyno exits on its own with `--no-tty`** (no manual `heroku ps:stop` needed, `heroku ps -a mindsync` showed zero stray `run.*` dynos after both script runs completed with exit 0). Update the access recipe mentally: the old "PTY hangs at `>>>`, must stop manually" caveat is specific to the plain `heroku run` (TTY) path, not the `--no-tty` one.

**Migration state confirmed current**: `alembic_version = pp7q8r9s0t1u`, live schema matches models exactly across `user_preferences`/`threads`/`thread_sources`/`todo_items`/`todo_completions`, and live `moment_type_enum` (22 labels) matches `MomentTypeEnum` 1:1. No drift anywhere in this batch's scope - a clean baseline snapshot as of 2026-09-02.

**CS-10 headline finding - the therapy-schedule persistence gap is population-wide, not Grace-specific.** Only 12 of 1810 `user_preferences` rows (0.66%) have a non-empty `therapy_schedule`; 99.7% have `next_session_date IS NULL`. Grace's own row: `therapy_frequency='biweekly'` persisted, but `therapy_schedule` (0 entries), `next_session_date` (NULL), and `custom_therapy_sessions` (0 entries) are ALL empty - the frequency mode field is the only thing that ever stuck. Session-driven notifications (THERAPY_APPOINTMENT/THERAPY_PREP/POST_SESSION_REFLECTION) DO fire correctly and on a steady cadence for the ~12-13 users who do have real schedule data - the bottleneck is 100% upstream at persistence, not in the generators. A narrower, more tractable repro population for `backend-api`: **7 users have a real non-empty `therapy_schedule` but `next_session_date` is NULL anyway** - the recompute/anchor-math half of the chain is silently failing for them specifically, unlike Grace where nothing persisted at all. The 2026-09-01 "invisible to eligibility gap" structural trap ([[project_cs02_add_therapy_date_false_push_2026_09_01]]) remains at 0 users. The v544 CS-02 guard against false ADD_THERAPY_DATE pushes for users who DO have a schedule is holding clean (0 such pushes since 2026-09-01 12:00 UTC across all 13 qualifying users).

**CS-07 - the repeat-todo feature (shipped 2026-07-17, `_maybe_reopen_repeating_todo`/`sweep_repeating_todos` in `app/crud/workspace.py`, wired into `app/api/v1/endpoints/workspace.py`) has exactly ONE live example in all of prod**, a single `daily` item (id=277) sitting DONE past its 1-day rollover window with `reactivated_at` still NULL at check time - genuinely ambiguous (window closed ~12h before the check; the mechanism is read-triggered only, no cron, so this could be "correctly waiting for next app open" or "actually stuck," undeterminable from data alone). What IS clearly established: the live mechanism is a **fixed day-offset from `done_at`** (daily=+1d/weekly=+7d/monthly=+30d), not the **calendar-boundary reset** (local midnight / user's week-start / 1st of month) Darek's 31.08 decision requires, and there is no background sweep - both are real gaps for `backend-api` regardless of whether the one live example is currently "stuck." Population size of 1 means "repeating items never recur, systemically" cannot be asserted from prod data - only "the feature is essentially unused."

**CS-01 - threads never merge, confirmed dataset-wide, not just on Grace's account.** `thread_sources` fan-out is exactly 1-per-thread across all 67 threads that have any source - zero threads have ever accumulated a second source. Only 6 of 1835 users have any open thread at all; Grace holds 26 of the 64 open threads system-wide (40%) and her own thread list shows 5 distinct clusters of 2-3 threads created within single-digit seconds of each other - a clean timestamped fingerprint of "one input burst -> several sibling threads" instead of one thread with sub-entries.

**CS-11 audit-prep data**: `REFLECTION_INVITATION` and `THERAPY_RELATIONSHIP_CHECKIN` reach ~1805-1806 of 1835 users roughly weekly and expire un-acted-on ~99.97% of the time (1 ACTED out of ~3371 each in 14 days) - sharper than the 2026-08-16 review's "~98% expire" estimate, strongest concrete "cycle is wrong" candidate found. `WEEKLY_SUMMARY_READY` reaches SENT only 33.5% of its 14-day volume, `MONTHLY_SUMMARY_READY` 50.8% - but the 14-day daily-volume series is dominated by four burst days (08-20, 08-23, 08-30, plus 08-24/08-31) that are ~85% of total volume against single/double-digit background days, so naive SENT/EXPIRED ratios or "average per day" reasoning will be distorted until whichever bulk/backfill-shaped job produces those spikes is traced (job-log tracing, not something derivable from this table - handed to backend-api/data-analytics, not root-caused here).

**FCM token coverage dropped**: 139/1835 (7.6%) now vs. 232/1730 (13.4%) at the 2026-07-03 audit ([[project_notification_moments_worker_registration_bug_2026_07_03]]) - both absolute count and share fell while the user base grew. Flagged, not diagnosed. Also: `users.last_login` is an unreliable "active user" proxy (only 21 users show as "active in last 30 days" by this column, fewer than the 63 users created in that same window) - do not use it for reach/DAU questions; route those to `posthog-data-extractor` instead.
