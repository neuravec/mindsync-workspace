🐋 Wee Woo Wee Woo, MindSync has a new home screen
Plus new before and after therapy cards, a rebuilt Character and an AI with a name.

The new MindSync is here!

Your whole therapy week now fits on one screen.

Between sessions, therapy tends to scatter. A to-do sits in one app, a habit you definitely did stays unchecked, and the question you came up with in the shower lives nowhere at all. So we did something about it, and now the part you need is one tap away.

Here's what's new:

Meet Main, your new home screen

MindSync now opens on Main, a screen of colorful tiles: your next session, your Therapy Agenda (topics for next time), to-dos, habits and Focus Areas (where you rate things like sleep from 1 to 10). Tap a tile to open it.

New before and after therapy cards

The After therapy card now has a chart, Your progress in therapy, which appears after three sessions. It reflects your entries (notes, recordings, Focus Area ratings) and doesn't grade your mental health or your therapist. The Before therapy card got clearer: a summary and numbered questions to bring.

Timeline and Workspace, tidied up

Timeline now puts your cards, notes and summaries in one list, sorted by date, and you can filter by type, like Reflect or weekly summaries. It comes in handy when your therapist asks when something started. Workspace got reshuffled, and the colors got brighter.

Character now grows from your words

We rebuilt Character around six strengths, like Courage and Insight, which grow from what you write and say, not from opening the app 40 times a day. (We love you, but no.) If Character gets a moment wrong, tap Not quite and it stops counting.

The AI in Reflect got a name

The AI in Reflect is now called Orca, MindSync's AI therapy assistant for the days between sessions. Orca isn't a therapist and doesn't replace yours. In Settings, the new What Orca remembers page lets you read every note Orca keeps about you and delete any of them.

+ a few smaller things

  1. A blue dot on a tile means something's waiting inside, like an open to-do.
  2. The habits tile shows your last seven days, so you can still check off a day you missed.
  3. You can now reread past Reflect conversations.

Update MindSync in the App Store or Google Play, and Main will be the first thing you see.
